# ShadowLab Branding Direction

## Product Name

Use the product name consistently:

- `ShadowLab`

Avoid alternate names unless a workspace subtitle is required, such as `Enterprise Ops` or `Security Ops`.

## Visual Direction

Preferred tone:

- precise
- tactical
- modern
- operator-focused

Avoid:

- cartoonish cyber imagery
- generic lock-only branding
- loud gradients that hurt dashboard readability

## Core Colors

- deep navy: `#10151d`
- operational blue: `#1d7df2`
- steel: `#243446`
- alert red: `#d64550`
- success green: `#2f9e67`
